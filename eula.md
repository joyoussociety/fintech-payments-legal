# End-User License Agreement — Fintech Payments

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Agreement

This End-User License Agreement governs the use of Fintech Payments, a private accounting application developed for Quickie Maximin LLC, doing business as Joyous Society Wine Co.

The application is intended exclusively for authorized internal business use.

## 2. Permitted Use

Authorized users may use Fintech Payments to:

- Import and review customer payment information from Excel spreadsheets.
- Retrieve invoice and customer information from QuickBooks Online.
- Match payments to existing customer invoices.
- Identify duplicate or previously recorded payments.
- Record approved customer payments in QuickBooks Online.
- Generate payment reconciliation reports.

## 3. Restrictions

Users may not distribute, sublicense, sell, or provide unauthorized third parties with access to the application.

The application must not be used to access QuickBooks accounts without appropriate authorization.

## 4. QuickBooks Integration

Fintech Payments uses Intuit's QuickBooks Online API to perform authorized accounting operations.

Users must maintain appropriate QuickBooks Online permissions and comply with applicable Intuit terms.

The application does not process credit card transactions, initiate bank transfers, or collect customer funds.

## 5. Data and Security

The application will use Intuit's supported authorization mechanisms for accessing QuickBooks Online.
