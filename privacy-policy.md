# Privacy Policy — Fintech Payments

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Overview

Fintech Payments is a private accounting application developed for internal use by Quickie Maximin LLC, doing business as Joyous Society Wine Co.

The application is intended to help authorized company personnel apply customer payments to existing invoices in QuickBooks Online.

Fintech Payments is not a payment-processing service and does not initiate customer charges or bank transfers.

## 2. Information Accessed

When authorized by the company, the application may access the following QuickBooks Online information:

- Customer names and identifiers
- Invoice numbers, dates, amounts, and outstanding balances
- Customer payment records and payment statuses
- Accounting information necessary to match and record customer payments

The application also processes Excel spreadsheets supplied by authorized company personnel.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely for internal accounting operations, including:

- Matching customer payments to outstanding invoices
- Identifying previously paid or partially paid invoices
- Detecting potential duplicate payments
- Preparing and recording customer payments in QuickBooks Online
- Producing payment reconciliation and exception reports

## 4. Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to operate on an authorized company Windows computer.

QuickBooks access will use Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization process rather than requiring users to provide their QuickBooks passwords to the application.

Financial information and authentication credentials will be handled using security measures appropriate to their sensitivity.

The application's final data-storage, credential-protection, and retention practices will be documented before production use.

## 5. Information Sharing

Fintech Payments is intended for internal company use. Information accessed through the application will not be sold or used for advertising.

Information will be exchanged with Intuit QuickBooks Online as necessary to perform authorized accounting operations.

## 6. Access and Control

Authorized company personnel control which spreadsheets are processed and whether payment transactions are submitted to QuickBooks Online.

Access to QuickBooks Online can be revoked through Intuit's applicable account and application authorization controls.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality, security practices, or applicable requirements.
